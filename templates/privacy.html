Privacy

This site has no forms, no accounts and no ads. If you allow it, Google Analytics counts page views and clicks on links to other sites. My hosting provider keeps a basic log of each request either way. This page explains what that means for you.

Last updated: [DATE GA IS INSTALLED]

What’s collected

Google Analytics

Only if you choose Allow. Until then, the Google Analytics code isn’t loaded and nothing is sent to Google. After you allow it, each time you open a page, your browser sends Google the page address and title, the page you came from, your browser and device type, screen size and language, and a random ID stored in a cookie so repeat visits can be counted. Google uses your IP address to estimate your city, region and country. Google says Google Analytics 4 doesn’t log or store IP addresses. When you click a link to another site, such as LinkedIn or GitHub, the address of that link is also sent.

Server logs

The site runs on Railway. For every request, the server writes a log line with your IP address, the time, the page requested and whether it loaded. I use these logs to fix errors and keep the site running.

Email

If you email me, I receive your address and whatever you write, and so does my email provider. Nothing on this site sends email for you. The email links just open your own mail app.

Your choices

On your first visit, a banner asks whether I can use Google Analytics. Nothing is sent to Google unless you choose Allow. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the site treats that as Decline and doesn’t ask. The site doesn’t respond to Do Not Track signals.

You can change your choice here at any time. If you switch to Decline, the site stops sending data and deletes its Google Analytics cookies from your browser, but it can’t remove data Google has already received.
