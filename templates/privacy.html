Privacy

This site has no forms, no accounts and no ads. It uses Google Analytics to count page views and clicks on links to other sites, and my hosting provider keeps a basic log of each request. This page explains what that means for you.

Last updated: [DATE GA IS INSTALLED]

What’s collected

Google Analytics

Each time you open a page, your browser sends Google the page address and title, the page you came from, your browser and device type, screen size and language, and a random ID stored in a cookie so repeat visits can be counted. Google uses your IP address to estimate your city, region and country. Google says Google Analytics 4 doesn’t log or store IP addresses. When you click a link to another site, such as LinkedIn or GitHub, the address of that link is also sent.

Server logs

The site runs on Railway. For every request, the server writes a log line with your IP address, the time, the page requested and whether it loaded. I use these logs to fix errors and keep the site running.

Email

If you email me, I receive your address and whatever you write, and so does my email provider. Nothing on this site sends email for you. The email links just open your own mail app.

Why

Your choices

There’s no cookie banner on this site, and it doesn’t respond to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals. You can still stop the analytics yourself:

  • Block cookies for this site in your browser settings, or use a private window.
  • Use a content blocker that blocks Google Analytics.
  • Install Google’s Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
